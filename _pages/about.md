I am an Accredited Statistician with a PhD in statistical machine learning and a Principal in the machine learning and mathematical optimisation team at Endgame Analytics. My PhD was completed under the supervision of [Dr. Ben Fulcher](http://www.benfulcher.com/) at The University of Sydney. I am interested in methods and applications of time-series features---properties which reduce a time series down to a scalar summary statistic (or set of summary statistics) which can then be used for machine learning applications---such as classification. My research largely focuses on systematically applying and comparing diverse time-series feature sets on applied problems and developing new efficient search algorithms for learning informative time-series features from data using symbolic regression via genetic programming.

A substantial portion of my work (and probably my deepest academic passion!) has involved building open-source statistical software. So far, I have developed [theft](https://hendersontrent.github.io/theft/) (Tools for Handling Extraction of Features from Time series), [theftdlc](https://hendersontrent.github.io/theftdlc/) (theft 'downloadable content'), [Rcatch22](https://github.com/hendersontrent/Rcatch22) (calculation of 22 CAnonical Time-series CHaracteristics), [correctR](https://hendersontrent.github.io/correctR/) (corrected test statistics for comparing machine learning models on correlated samples), [fable.gam](https://github.com/hendersontrent/fable.gam) (fable extension for doing time-series modelling with generalised additive models), [normaliseR](https://github.com/hendersontrent/normaliseR) (rescaling methods for numerical vectors and time-series features), [PosteriorPlots.jl](https://github.com/hendersontrent/PosteriorPlots.jl) (graphical tools for Bayesian inference and posterior predictive checks), [QuasiGLM.jl](https://github.com/hendersontrent/QuasiGLM.jl) (adjustments for Poisson and Binomial Generalised Linear Models to their quasi equivalents for dispersed data), and [gate](https://github.com/hendersontrent/gate) (Geospatial Analysis and Temporal Engineering).

Outside of my research, I am also working on [GeneralizedAdditiveModels.jl](https://github.com/hendersontrent/GeneralizedAdditiveModels.jl)---an ambitious attempt to build [generalised additive models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_additive_model) in native Julia using as similar of an interface as possible to R's incredible [mgcv](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/mgcv/index.html) package.

### Professional experience

I am an Accredited Statistician and a Principal in the machine learning and mathematical optimisation team at [Endgame Analytics](https://www.endgameanalytics.com.au) where I work on time-series problems for the energy sector. Prior to this role, I was the Lead Data Scientist at the consulting firm Nous Group where I worked for nearly 8 years.
